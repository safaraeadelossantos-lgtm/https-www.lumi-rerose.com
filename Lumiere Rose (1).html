About Lumière Rose

Lumière Rose began with one idea: every dream has a sound, and every sound can be worn. Our designers drape silk over sheet music and name each piece after a musical term, so creativity becomes a silhouette.

We design for elegance that feels effortless, femininity that feels powerful, and confidence that feels earned. Soft, durable fabrics and timeless shapes turn a dream into something you can put on.
